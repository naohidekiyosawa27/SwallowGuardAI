# M0-R3 waveform QAT: numerical research candidate

Status: numerical validation passed; physical inference and independent validation pending. RESEARCH MODE. Not a clinical risk model. Do not use for swallowing-safety clearance or treatment decisions.

- Data: authorized D4 v2 healthy task clips, paired sound and upper/lower EMG. Original train/validation/exposed-regression split: 26/6/6 participants; 2722/602/726 clips. No prototype optical, PVDF or respiratory measurements.
- Input: 2 seconds, 2 kHz, `[1,4000,3]`, channel order sound/upper EMG/lower EMG. Upstream D4 filtering/resampling is specified in the adapter. Per window/channel, subtract mean, divide by centred peak magnitude and multiply by 0.9. Reject nonfinite/flat windows; retain raw signals separately. This representation removes absolute amplitude and cannot certify sensor quality.
- Architecture: Conv1D8/k5/stride2 → ReLU6 → Conv1D16/k5/stride2 → ReLU6 → mean pooling → Dense16/ReLU6 → Dense1 → sigmoid. 1073 parameters. QAT fixed ranges and integer conversion are in `train/qat_models.py`.
- Output: swallow-task event probability, threshold 0.5. Negative class is cough/throat-clearing, not unrestricted background or clinically safe swallowing.
- Actual full-INT8 model: 5800 bytes; SHA256 `ed486cdfc5a06578a444615a1bf7c0b3f2b745a424d149a21d5db2c3bdd1eeda`; input scale 0.007843137718737125, zero point 0; output scale 0.00390625, zero point -128. No float tensors; five audited operators.
- Selected on reused validation: coverage 100%, F1 0.93906, max float-shadow probability error 0.03995, threshold agreement 99.668%. Exposed regression F1 0.91951, participant-cluster 95% interval 0.86571–0.96063. These are internal event-centred results, not external or clinical validation.
- Physical latency, arena, power, calibration of probability and continuous-event accuracy are unknown. No hardware invocation is claimed. Retain independent quality/range gates and verify the exact artifact before a disconnected bench run.
